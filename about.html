About This Blog

Who writes this, why it exists, and what you'll find here.

The Writer

Hi, I'm Alex Chen. I write about attention, craft, technology, and the quiet art of doing meaningful work in a noisy world.

By day, I build software. By night, I read too many books and write essays that start as notes to myself.

Why This Exists

I started this blog because I wanted a place that respects the reader. No pop-ups, no tracking, no algorithmic feed, no engagement bait. Just writing that earns your time.

The internet is full of content. It's not full of craft.

What You'll Find

  • Essays — Long-form pieces on attention, creativity, technology, culture.
  • Reading notes — What I'm learning from books worth re-reading.
  • Occasional experiments — Small projects, tools, or ideas I'm playing with.

The Newsletter

The best writing goes to email subscribers first. No spam, no tracking, no growth hacks. Just the next essay when it's ready.

Principles

  1. Writing first. Every design decision serves the reading experience.
  2. Respect the reader. No dark patterns, no tracking, no forced interactions.
  3. Own the relationship. Email is the only channel I control.
  4. Static by default. Zero dependencies, zero build, zero surprises.
  5. Craft shows. Small details signal quality before a word is read.

Technical Notes

This blog is hand-coded static HTML/CSS/JS. No framework, no build step, no database. It deploys with a git push.

Fonts: Libre Bodoni (headings) + Public Sans (body), self-hosted in production.

Icons: Lucide (SVG, inline).

Analytics: Plausible (privacy-friendly, optional).
