Privacy Policy

How we handle your data. Spoiler: we barely collect any.

Last updated: September 2026

The Short Version

We don't track you. We don't sell your data. We don't use cookies for advertising. The only personal data we collect is your email address — and only if you voluntarily subscribe to the newsletter.

What We Collect

Newsletter Subscriptions

When you subscribe, we collect your email address. That's it. No name, no location, no tracking pixels in emails.

Your email is stored with our newsletter provider (Buttondown/ConvertKit/Beehiiv — see footer for current provider). We never export, share, or sell this list.

Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics — a privacy-friendly, GDPR-compliant alternative to Google Analytics. It uses no cookies, collects no personal data, and doesn't track individuals across sites. We see aggregate page views and referrers. That's all.

You can opt out via your browser's Do Not Track setting or by using a content blocker.

Server Logs

Our static host (Netlify/Vercel/GitHub Pages/Cloudflare Pages) logs standard access data (IP, user agent, timestamp) for security and abuse prevention. These logs are retained per the host's policy (typically 30 days) and not accessible to us in identifiable form.

What We Don't Do

  • No third-party tracking scripts (Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, etc.)
  • No advertising cookies
  • No fingerprinting
  • No email open tracking (no invisible pixels in newsletters)
  • No link click tracking in emails
  • No data sharing with advertisers
  • No profiling or automated decision-making

Your Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and similar regulations, you have the right to:

  • Access your data — email us and we'll tell you what we have
  • Rectify incorrect data — reply to any newsletter
  • Erasure — unsubscribe link in every email, or email us
  • Portability — we'll export your data in a standard format
  • Object to processing — unsubscribe and we stop

Third-Party Links

This site links to external sites (Twitter, GitHub, etc.). We're not responsible for their privacy practices. Check their policies.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll note it in the newsletter. The "Last updated" date above will change.
